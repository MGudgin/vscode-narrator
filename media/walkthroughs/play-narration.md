# Play a narration

With speech enabled and a narration open:

- Press **▶ Play** in the banner to read the whole narration aloud, section by section.
- Press the **▶** button next to any section heading to jump speech to that section.
- Use **⏸ Pause** to hold the current utterance, **■ Stop** to cancel and rewind to the top.

Sections still streaming are honoured — playback waits for the next sentence rather than skipping the section.

Try running **Code Narration: Open Narration** on the active file, then **Code Narration: Speak Narration** from the command palette.
